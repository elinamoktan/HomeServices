Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I book an appointment with a service provider?

To book an appointment, simply browse our verified service providers, select the one that matches your needs, choose your preferred date and time slot, and confirm your booking. You'll receive instant confirmation and reminders as your appointment approaches.

Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment?

Yes! You can reschedule or cancel your appointment up to 2 hours before the scheduled time. Go to your appointments page, select the appointment you wish to modify, and choose your preferred option. For urgent cancellations, contact our support team.

How are service providers verified?

All our service providers undergo a rigorous verification process including background checks, document verification, and skill assessments. We also monitor customer ratings and reviews to ensure consistent quality service.

What if I'm not satisfied with the service?

Your satisfaction is our priority. If you're not happy with the service, contact us within 24 hours of the appointment completion. We'll work with you and the service provider to resolve the issue, which may include a partial refund or arranging a re-service.

How do I rate and review a service provider?

After your appointment is completed, you'll receive a notification to rate your experience. You can provide a star rating and detailed feedback. Your reviews help other customers make informed decisions and help us maintain service quality.
