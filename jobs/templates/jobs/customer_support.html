Frequently Asked Questions

Click on any question to get a detailed answer

How do I book an appointment with a service provider?

To book an appointment, simply browse our verified service providers using the search bar or categories. Once you find a suitable provider, select their profile to view their services, availability, and pricing. Choose your preferred date and time slot from the calendar, add any specific notes for the provider, and confirm your booking. You'll receive instant email confirmation and reminders as your appointment approaches.

Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment?

Yes, absolutely! You can easily reschedule or cancel your appointment directly from your "My Appointments" dashboard. We allow modifications up to 2 hours before the scheduled time without any penalty. Simply navigate to your appointment, click on the "Reschedule" or "Cancel" button, and follow the prompts. For urgent cancellations within the 2-hour window or any issues, please contact our support team immediately.

How are service providers verified?

At AppointMate, we prioritize your safety and satisfaction. All our service providers undergo a stringent multi-step verification process. This includes comprehensive background checks, verification of professional licenses and certifications (where applicable), and skill assessments. We also continuously monitor customer ratings and reviews to ensure consistent quality and adherence to our high standards.

What if I'm not satisfied with the service?

Your satisfaction is paramount to us. If you are not completely happy with a service you received, please contact our support team within 24 hours of the appointment completion. We will thoroughly investigate your concerns and work collaboratively with you and the service provider to resolve the issue to your satisfaction, which may involve a partial refund, re-service arrangements, or other appropriate actions.

How do I rate and review a service provider?

After your appointment is successfully completed, you will receive a notification prompting you to rate and review your experience. You can provide a star rating (from 1 to 5) and leave detailed feedback about the service provider's professionalism, quality of work, and punctuality. Your valuable reviews help other customers make informed decisions and contribute to maintaining our platform's high service standards.
